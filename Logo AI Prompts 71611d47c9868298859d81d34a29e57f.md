# Logo AI Prompts

**After you've chosen your domain name, paste this in the same ChatGPT conversation:**

---

Great! I've decided on this domain name: [YOUR CHOSEN DOMAIN]

Now I need a logo for this brand.

Create a simple, professional logo concept that:

- Reflects the brand vibe we discussed
- Works for my target audience
- Is clean and modern
- Would work well on both a website header and product packaging
- Uses 2-3 colors maximum

Please describe:

1. The logo concept
2. Color palette (give me hex codes)
3. Typography style
4. Why this design works for my brand

Then generate the logo image for me.

---

**✅ AFTER CHATGPT GENERATES YOUR LOGO:**

To make changes:

- "Make it more minimal"
- "Try different colors - use [color] and [color]"
- "Give me 3 different versions"
- "Remove the icon, just make it a text logo"

To download:

- Right-click the image → Save image as
- Upload to Shopify in Settings → Brand

Remove BG:

- [https://www.remove.bg/upload](https://www.remove.bg/upload)

---

💡 LOGO TIPS:

- Simpler is better - think Nike, Apple, Adidas
- Your logo will be tiny on mobile - keep it readable
- Test it: Can you recognize it when it's the size of your thumbnail?
- You can always refine it later - don't overthink it
