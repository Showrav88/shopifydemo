# Domain Name AI Prompt

## **Copy this prompt and use it on ChatGPT ([chat.openai.com](http://chat.openai.com/)) or Claude ([claude.ai](http://claude.ai/)):**

I need 15 brandable domain name ideas for my e-commerce store.

Store niche/products: [FILL IN: e.g., "fitness accessories" or "home decor" or "general store"]

Brand vibe I want: [FILL IN: e.g., "modern and minimal" or "luxurious" or "fun and young" or "trustworthy"]

Target audience: [FILL IN: e.g., "young Indian professionals" or "moms" or "college students"]

**Requirements:**

- Short (6-10 characters preferred)
- Easy to spell and pronounce
- Memorable and brandable
- Works for Indian audience
- No hyphens or numbers
- .com or .in extensions

**Give me:**

1. 10 invented/coined names (like "Zappos" or "Myntra")
2. 5 compound word names (like "SnapDeal" or "ShopClues")

For each name, briefly explain why it works.

---

**✅ HOW TO CHECK IF YOUR DOMAIN IS AVAILABLE:**

Method 1 (Easiest):
During Shopify signup, type your domain name - it shows availability instantly

Method 2:
Go to [domains.shopify.com](http://domains.shopify.com/) and search

Method 3:
Use [GoDaddy.com](http://godaddy.com/) or [Namecheap.com](http://namecheap.com/) domain search

---

**💡 DOMAIN NAMING TIPS:**

- Shorter is better (under 10 characters ideal)
- Avoid numbers and hyphens
- Make sure it's easy to spell when you say it out loud
- .com is best, but .in works great for Indian stores
- Don't stress - your marketing matters more than your domain name
