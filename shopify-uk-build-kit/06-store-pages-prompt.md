# Store Pages Prompt — UK Legal & Content

Generate all required store pages in one AI session. Copy each section below as a separate message, or combine into one prompt.

---

## Prompt 1 — About Us

```
Brand: [INSERT BRAND NAME]
Store: UK online fashion — men's and women's clothing and accessories
No physical location. Ships UK-wide.

Write an About Us page (3–4 paragraphs):
- Why we started — making quality everyday fashion accessible online in the UK
- What we believe — inclusive sizing, honest pricing, sustainable where possible
- What we sell — men's and women's apparel and accessories
- Our promise — quality products, fast UK delivery, hassle-free returns

Tone: authentic, warm, professional
Leave placeholders: [COMPANY NAME], [YEAR FOUNDED], [CONTACT EMAIL]
Do NOT mention this is an internal test store.
```

---

## Prompt 2 — Privacy Policy (UK GDPR)

```
Write a Privacy Policy for [BRAND NAME], a UK e-commerce store selling men's and women's clothing and accessories online. No physical store.

Must comply with UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018.

Include sections:
1. Who we are (data controller)
2. What personal data we collect (name, email, address, payment, cookies, analytics)
3. Legal basis for processing (contract, consent, legitimate interest)
4. How we use your data
5. Cookies and tracking (Shopify, Google Analytics if used)
6. Third-party services (Shopify Payments, shipping carriers, email provider)
7. Data retention periods
8. Your rights (access, rectification, erasure, portability, objection)
9. How to complain to the ICO (ico.org.uk)
10. Contact details

Placeholders: [COMPANY NAME], [REGISTERED ADDRESS OR "Online only — no physical premises"], [CONTACT EMAIL], [DPO EMAIL IF APPLICABLE]
Tone: clear, readable — not dense legalese.
```

## Prompt 3 — Terms of Service (UK)

```
Write Terms of Service for [BRAND NAME], UK online fashion store (men's and women's clothing and accessories).

Include sections:
1. Agreement to terms
2. Account registration
3. Products and pricing (prices in GBP, include VAT where applicable)
4. Orders and acceptance
5. Payment (Shopify Payments, accepted card types)
6. Shipping and delivery (UK only)
7. Returns and refunds (reference Consumer Rights Act 2015 — 14-day cancellation for distance sales)
8. Intellectual property
9. Limitation of liability
10. Governing law: England and Wales
11. Dispute resolution
12. Changes to terms
13. Contact information

Placeholders: [COMPANY NAME], [CONTACT EMAIL], [COMPANY NUMBER IF REGISTERED]
```

## Prompt 4 — Shipping Policy

```
Write a Shipping Policy for [BRAND NAME], UK-only online fashion store. No physical store or click-and-collect.

Include:
1. Delivery areas (United Kingdom — England, Scotland, Wales, Northern Ireland)
2. Shipping methods: Standard (3–5 business days), Express (1–2 business days)
3. Shipping costs: [STANDARD RATE e.g. £3.95], free delivery over [THRESHOLD e.g. £50]
4. Order processing time: [1–2 business days]
5. Tracking: provided via email when order ships
6. Delivery issues: lost/damaged parcels — contact us within [X] days
7. No international shipping currently
8. Bank holidays may delay dispatch

Placeholders: [STANDARD RATE], [EXPRESS RATE], [FREE DELIVERY THRESHOLD], [CONTACT EMAIL]
Tone: clear, customer-friendly.
```

## Prompt 5 — Returns & Refunds Policy

```
Write a Returns and Refunds Policy for [BRAND NAME], UK online fashion store.

Must align with UK Consumer Rights Act 2015 and Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013.

Include:
1. 14-day cancellation right (distance selling — unworn, tags attached, original packaging)
2. Return window: [30 DAYS] from delivery for exchanges/refunds
3. How to start a return (email [CONTACT EMAIL] or returns portal)
4. Refund method and timing (within 14 days of receiving returned item)
5. Return shipping: [customer pays / free returns]
6. Exchanges: size/colour swap process
7. Faulty or damaged items: full refund including return postage
8. Non-returnable items: underwear, earrings, personalised items (if applicable)
9. Sale items: [same policy / final sale — specify]

Placeholders: [RETURN WINDOW], [CONTACT EMAIL], [RETURN ADDRESS OR "We will email you a return label"]
```

## How to Add Pages in Shopify

1. Online Store → **Pages** → Add page
2. Paste content, set page title and URL handle
3. Add to footer menu: Online Store → Navigation → Footer menu

## Required Pages Checklist

| Page | URL handle | In footer? |
|------|-------------|------------|
| About Us | `/pages/about-us` | Yes |
| Privacy Policy | `/pages/privacy-policy` | Yes |

## Legal Review Note

These AI-generated policies are a **strong starting point**. Before any public launch, have them reviewed by a UK solicitor or use a service like [TermsFeed](https://www.termsfeed.com/) or [Shopify's policy generator](https://www.shopify.com/uk/tools/policy-generator).

For **internal testing only**, the generated policies are sufficient to populate the store realistically.
