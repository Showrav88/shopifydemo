# Brand & Domain Name Prompt

Copy the prompt below into **ChatGPT** ([chat.openai.com](https://chat.openai.com)) or **Claude** ([claude.ai](https://claude.ai)).

---

## Prompt (copy from here)

```
I am building a UK e-commerce store on Shopify for men's and women's clothing and accessories. The store has no physical location and will be used internally by our team to test products we are building.

Generate 15 brandable domain name ideas.

Brand positioning:
- Modern British fashion — accessible, stylish, not luxury
- Sells both men's and women's apparel plus accessories (belts, scarves, caps, bags)
- Target audience: UK shoppers aged 22–45 who shop online for everyday and smart-casual wear
- Tone: clean, confident, inclusive — not gendered or old-fashioned

Requirements:
- Short (6–12 characters preferred)
- Easy to spell and pronounce for UK English speakers
- Memorable and brandable (not generic like "UKFashionShop")
- Works with .co.uk or .com extensions
- No hyphens, numbers, or trademark conflicts with major UK brands (ASOS, Next, Zara, etc.)

Deliver:
1. 10 invented/coined names (e.g. "Veylo", "Modra", "Threadly")
2. 5 compound word names (e.g. "WearHouse", "ClothCo")

For each name provide:
- Domain suggestion (.co.uk and .com)
- One-line rationale
- Brand vibe in 3 adjectives
```

## How to Check Availability

| Method | Steps |
|--------|-------|
| **During Shopify signup** | Type the name — Shopify shows availability instantly |
| **Shopify Domains** | Search at [domains.shopify.com](https://domains.shopify.com/) |
| **WHOIS lookup** | Use [nominet.uk](https://www.nominet.uk/lookup/) for .co.uk |

## Naming Tips for This Project

- `.co.uk` signals UK trust — prefer it for this store
- Avoid names that sound like existing high-street brands
- For internal testing, a `.myshopify.com` subdomain is fine — buy a domain only if the store goes public
- Pick a name the whole team can say in stand-ups without spelling it out
